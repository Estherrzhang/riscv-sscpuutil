[[sscpuutil]]
=== CPU Utilization Counter Extensions, Version 1.0

==== Introduction

The CPU Utilization Counter specification (Sscpuutil) defines two extensions:
ext:smnacttime[] for active-time counting and ext:ssacttime[] for
supervisor-level access and virtualization.

Modern operating-system schedulers require a frequency-invariant measure of
CPU utilization to drive dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS)
governors and task-placement decisions. Computing the effective operating
frequency requires two counters that advance only while the hart is actively
executing: a _delivered_ counter that counts actual clock cycles, and a
_reference_ counter that counts at a fixed frequency. The ratio of their
increments over an observation window yields the frequency-scaling factor
without any query to platform firmware.
